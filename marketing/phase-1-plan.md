# Tessera posting plan · Phase 1: pre-launch (2 weeks)

Goal: a waiting list. Every post ends on the same ask: **Comment TILE** and you get the link.
Account is faceless. The mural is the face; you speak as voiceover only.

Placeholders in `{{double braces}}` need real facts before posting. Never fill them with guesses.

Kit boards referenced below live on the Tessera Social Kit canvas.

---

## How to produce each format

**Carousel (1080×1350)**
1. Open the board, edit the text in place, export each slide as PNG.
2. Upload in order. First slide is the cover.

**Story (1080×1920)**
1. Export the board as PNG.
2. In Instagram, add the real sticker (poll, question, link) on the dashed frame, then delete nothing else.

**Reel or TikTok (9:16, under 30 seconds)**
1. Record the scene: open the scene board, press Play, use macOS screen recording (Cmd+Shift+5) on just the board. Record 20 seconds; the scenes loop.
2. For app footage, open the live site on your phone and use the phone's screen recorder, or record the browser at a 9:16 window size.
3. In CapCut or the Instagram editor: lay the clips, add the overlay PNG (export overlay boards with the Preview "frame" setting at 0 so only the text shows), add voiceover, end on the end card for 2 seconds.
4. Captions on screen, always. Many people watch muted.

**Timing**
Pick two slots to test, for example 12:00 and 19:00 your time. Post Reels in one, carousels in the other. After week 1, keep the slot with more saves and shares.

## Rules for every post
- No invented numbers, quotes or contributor stories. Sample tiles are labelled as samples.
- No em dashes. Plain, calm words. No hype.
- Max 5 hashtags on Instagram.
- Reply to every "TILE" comment with the waiting-list link {{waiting list link}}.
- Show a real person's tile only with their permission.

1. **Waiting list:** set one up (a simple form is fine) and put its link in {{waiting list link}}.
2. **Launch date:** pick it and replace {{launch date}}.
3. **Your first tile:** make it on the live site with a real voice note. Reels 5 and 6 and Carousel 2 depend on it.
4. **Brand voice:** this plan is written without `voice.md`. Add it and I'll rewrite the captions to match.

Phases 2 (launch week) and 3 (weekly rhythm) follow once you approve this one.
