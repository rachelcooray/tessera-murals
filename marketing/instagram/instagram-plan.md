## Create the account (about 20 minutes, on your phone)

1. **Make a separate account, not your personal one.** In the Instagram app, open your profile, tap your username at the top, then **Add account** → **Create new account**. Use an email you'll keep for Tessera.
2. **Name and handle.** Name: `Tessera · Hidden mosaics` (the name field is searchable, so the words help people find you). Handle: the one you chose above.
3. **Profile picture.** Upload `profile-picture.png`.
4. **Switch to a professional account.** Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account. Choose **Business**, category **Art** or **Gift shop**. This gives you insights, a contact button and scheduling. Instagram moves these menus around now and then, so if a step looks different, search the settings for "professional".
5. **Bio.** Paste this (it's under the 150-character limit):

   > Your photo, made from other people's art.
   > Everyone paints one square. Nobody sees it until the reveal.
   > Founding mosaics ↓

6. **Link.** Edit profile → Links → add your waiting list link, titled "Join the founding list".
7. **Contact button.** Add your Tessera email so people can tap Email.
8. **Turn on two-factor authentication** (Settings → Accounts Centre → Password and security). Losing a brand account is painful.
9. **Don't follow anyone or invite friends yet.** Post the launch grid first (below) so the page looks complete when people arrive.

## The launch grid: one hidden mosaic

The first nine posts make **one picture together**: Antelope Canyon lit by a sunbeam (10,800 squares, Unsplash photo by Madhu Shesharam; two more scenery sets in `grid/sets/`), split into nine pieces. Each post's cover is one numbered piece. As you post, the picture builds. Once all nine are up, the profile shows the whole thing, the way a Tessera mosaic is revealed. See `marketing/instagram/grid/profile-mockup.png`.

```
 newest  ┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
         │ 9 Painted blind  │ 8 Paint on your  │ 7 How it works   │
         │   (Reel 1)       │   phone (carousel)│   (carousel)     │
         ├──────────────────┼──────────────────┼──────────────────┤
         │ 6 Gift ideas     │ 5 Paint a tile   │ 4 Paint as many  │
         │   (carousel)     │   (Reel 3)       │   (single)       │
         ├──────────────────┼──────────────────┼──────────────────┤
         │ 3 Questions      │ 2 Sizes          │ 1 The reveal     │
 oldest  │   (carousel)     │   (single)       │   (Reel 2)       │
         └──────────────────┴──────────────────┴──────────────────┘
```

The piece files are in `marketing/instagram/grid/`, named by post number (`grid-1-the-reveal.png` to `grid-9-painted-blind.png`). Each is 1080×1440 (3:4), the shape Instagram's profile grid uses.

**How each kind of post uses its piece:**
- **Carousel:** the piece is slide 1, then the carousel's own slides follow. Instagram fits every slide to the first one's shape, which trims a sliver off the sides of the other slides. Their margins are wide enough that nothing important is lost.
- **Single post:** the piece is slide 1, and the actual post (for example the Sizes card) is slide 2. Swiping reveals it.
- **Reel:** post the video, then tap **Edit cover → Add from camera roll** and choose the piece. The grid shows the cover, not the video.

**Rules that keep the picture whole:**
1. **Post in number order, 1 to 9.** Post 1 ends up bottom-right and post 9 top-left.
2. **Ideally post all nine on launch day,** a few minutes apart, so nobody sees half a picture. If you can't, post them in rows of three.
3. **Don't pin anything.** Pinned posts jump to the front and break the picture. (This replaces the pinning advice from earlier.)
4. **After the nine, post in threes.** Each new post pushes the grid along by one, so single posts shift the picture out of line. Adding a full row of three at a time keeps it aligned. The second row of three can be a new strip, for example three gift ideas.
5. **Never delete one of the nine.** Archive it instead if you must, though even that shifts the grid.

Captions for all nine are in `marketing/instagram/grid/captions.md`. Label-free versions of the pieces are in `grid/no-labels/`.

**All nine are ready** except your Reel 3 screen recording. For each post, upload the grid piece first, then its slides from `posts/`.

## Rules for every post

- No made-up customers, quotes, numbers or reviews. The sample mosaic is called a sample.
- Don't say "every square is painted by a person". Say "hand-painted by your people and Tessera artists".
- Show someone's tile, face or photo only with their written permission.
- No AI-made images, ever. That's the whole promise.
- Plain, warm words. No hype.
- 3 to 5 hashtags. Captions on every video.
- Don't promise artist payouts by a date until payouts exist.
