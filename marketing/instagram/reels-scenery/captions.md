# Scenery reels and short clips

All 1080×1920, 30 fps, made from real Unsplash photos (credits below and on screen). The squares are a sample from our generator, not real guests' tiles.

## Reels (with voice, 15 to 18 seconds)

### reel-canyon-fill.mp4 (18 s)
Squares fill in one by one, slowly then all at once, until Antelope Canyon appears.
**Cover:** frame at about 12 s (the full picture).
> 10,800 squares. Imagine each one painted by a different person, and none of them ever saw the picture. ✨
>
> Until the reveal.

### reel-jellyfish-guess.mp4 (16 s)
Starts so close you only see squares, then pulls out to a swarm of jellyfish.
**Cover:** the first frame (just squares) so people have to watch.
> Guess what it is before it ends. 👀 Comment your guess first, no cheating.
>
> Every one of those squares could be painted by a different person. Make one from your photo. Link in bio.
>
> Sample photo: Joel Filipe on Unsplash.
>
> #hiddenmosaic #tessera #guesswhat #mosaicart #satisfying

### reel-firefall-one-square.mp4 (15 s)
One square gets painted, then the camera pulls back through 10,800 of them to the Yosemite firefall.
**Cover:** the frame at about 11.5 s (the whole waterfall).
> This is one square. One person painted it, and they never saw the rest. 🔥
>
> That's how every Tessera mosaic is made: your photo, painted square by square by the people who love you, hidden until the reveal.
