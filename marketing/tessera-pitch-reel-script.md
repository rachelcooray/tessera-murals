## Notes before showing it

- The mosaic in the video is a sample made for the demo, and it's labelled as one. Say so if anyone asks.
- Prices shown: $45, $95, $145 and $195 for 10,000 to 40,000 tiles. "About two thirds" is what's left of each order after card fees and the artist share (64 to 66%, see docs/pricing.md).
- There are no customers or sales figures in the video, because there aren't any yet. When you have real ones, they're the strongest thing to add.
- If you're asking for something specific (money, time, help), add it in the last 5 seconds: "I'm asking for [YOUR ASK] to [WHAT IT PAYS FOR]."
- The music bed is a simple generated pad. For Instagram, you can swap it for a track from Instagram's own music library when you post.
