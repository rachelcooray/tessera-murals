# Tessera pitch reel · voiceover script

Video: `marketing/tessera-pitch-reel.mp4` (59 s, 1080×1920, silent track). Record this over it in CapCut or iMovie, and add soft music under it at low volume. Read slowly; each line sits on its scene.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 4 s | One painted square | "Four hundred people. One colour each. None of them sees the picture." |
| 4 to 10 s | Zoom out to the whole mosaic | "Until the reveal." (pause, let the zoom play) |
| 10 to 15 s | TESSERA | "This is Tessera. Your photo, made from other people's art." |
| 15 to 18 s | I · Upload | "You upload one photo. Only you see it whole." |
| 18 to 21 s | II · One colour | "Everyone you invite gets one square and one colour. Never the picture." |
| 21 to 24 s | III · Paint | "They paint it by hand. Friends first, then Tessera artists fill the rest." |
| 24 to 28 s | IV · Reveal | "On the day, it's revealed. One tile, then the whole picture." |
| 28 to 34 s | The real app | "And they can paint it right on their phone. This is the real tool, already built." |
| 34 to 40 s | Who it's for | "It's for weddings, big birthdays, anniversaries, teams, and families remembering someone." |
| 40 to 47 s | How it makes money | "The customer pays from forty-nine dollars. Contributors pay nothing. Artists share thirty percent for filling open squares, and Tessera keeps seventy, before fees and running costs." |
| 47 to 53 s | Already built | "The ordering, the invites, the review, the paint tool and the reveal are already built. Next is the real database and artist payouts." |
| 53 to 59 s | Close | "Then the first twenty founding mosaics. Tessera. Hidden mosaics, made by people." |

## Notes before showing it

- The mosaic in the video is a sample made for the demo, and it's labelled as one. Say so if anyone asks.
- The prices are the site's current placeholder prices. Change them in the video if you settle on different ones.
- There are no customers or sales figures in the video, because there aren't any yet. When you have real ones, they're the strongest thing to add.
- If you're asking for something specific (money, time, help), say it in the last 5 seconds: "I'm asking for [YOUR ASK] to [WHAT IT PAYS FOR]."
